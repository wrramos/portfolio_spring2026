favorite makeup brands

I work at a makeup store so I'm always surrounded by people asking me for my recomendations. NARS can be a bit
expensive but it's worth it for the quality of their products! Fenty has a really good shade range making it easier
to find a shade that works for you! Morphe and Juvia's Place also have great products for a lower price point! Let me
know if you ever need any recomendations for makeup products! I love talking about makeup and sharing my favorites!
