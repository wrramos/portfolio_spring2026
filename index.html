About Wendy

Wendy is a First Generation Salvadorean-American born and raised in the Bay Area. She is currently a sophomore at San Francisco State University where she is majoring in Visual Communications. She has a love for makeup, design, and photography. She first started her design journey in High School when she joined a design program called "CMMA", which stands for "Community MultiMedia Academy". In this program she learned about different design softwares like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator and touched a bit on web design and Java Script.
